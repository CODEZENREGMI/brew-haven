Our story

A corner of calm in a hurried city

Brew Haven started with a secondhand roaster, a borrowed espresso machine, and a stubborn belief that a good cup of coffee can change the shape of a day. Ten years later, we're still on the same corner — just a little busier.

We work directly with farmers we've visited, roast in small batches behind the bar, and bake every pastry before sunrise. No shortcuts, no mystery syrups. Just coffee made by people who care, for people who notice.

  • Direct trade

    Long-term relationships with growers, and prices that reflect their craft.

  • Roasted in-house

    Small batches, roasted weekly, never older than ten days on the shelf.

  • Baked at dawn

    Croissants laminated by hand. Buns glazed while the city wakes up.
